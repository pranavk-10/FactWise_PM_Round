# Open Questions — Must Resolve Before Production

## Clinical workflow
1. What precisely constitutes fever, and what measurement method/unit/precision is approved?
2. Does “3 full days without fever” mean three calendar days, three complete 24-hour periods, or a minimum elapsed duration?
3. What readings and observation frequency are required to count a day as fever-free? How are missing/late readings treated?
4. Does medication that reduces fever affect eligibility, and how is it documented?
5. What happens when a doctor must see a patient before the nurse records temperature?
6. Who can approve discharge and what documentation is required?

## Operations
7. Are rounds tied to shifts or a fixed facility-local time?
8. Do nurses/doctors have assigned patients or shared queues?
9. How should urgent tasks be prioritized, and who owns unresolved tasks at shift handover?
10. What constitutes an occupied bed during cleaning/turnover?
11. What notification channel is approved for staff alerts?

## Outcome reporting
12. Is the 15% mortality benchmark a facility-wide benchmark, disease-specific cohort benchmark, or another measure?
13. What is the denominator and reporting period? Are transfers, readmissions, and deaths after transfer included?
14. What minimum case count should trigger an alert, and who reviews it?
15. What level of risk adjustment or case-mix stratification is required?

## Technology and governance
16. Which hosting/data residency options are approved?
17. What identity provider and staff device policy are available?
18. What retention, access logging, export and downtime requirements apply?
19. What accessibility and language requirements should be prioritized?
20. Who owns clinical rule approval, production sign-off, support, and incident response?
